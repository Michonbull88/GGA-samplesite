ABOUT GGA

An African organisation for governance that works.

Good Governance Africa is an independent African governance organisation working to strengthen governance systems and institutions across the continent.

WHO WE ARE

Independent.
African.
Evidence-led.

We believe good governance is essential to Africa's long-term prosperity, security and human development.

GGA brings research, advocacy, convening and applied programmes together around practical governance challenges. Our work is designed to be useful to decision-makers, institutions, civil society, communities and partners.
